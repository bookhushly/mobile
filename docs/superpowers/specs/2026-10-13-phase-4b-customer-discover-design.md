# Phase 4b — Customer discover: explore, search, listing detail, saved (design)

**Status:** decided 2026-10-13 under the owner's standing instruction to make every call ("Yes begin immediately"). Rulings in §2 give the reason for each call and what it costs if wrong. **Branch:** `feat/phase-4b-customer-discover`. **Builds on:** Phase 4a (customer tabs, bookings, kit). **Requirements:** FR-2.1, FR-2.2, FR-2.3 (read-only), FR-2.13 (save only), FR-2.17. **Backend:** verified against web `origin/main` @ `30c7da90` + the prod schema snapshot; summarised in `docs/BACKEND_STATUS.md` §7/§9 by this phase. **References:** `docs/design-references/2026-10-13-phase-4b-customer-discover-mobbin.md`. **Out:** booking, availability calendars, checkout, payment, reviews submission, waitlist, follow organizer (4c / Phase 5).

## 1. Goal and success

A customer opens the app and can:

- browse hotels, serviced apartments and events;
- search by name or place;
- filter and sort the results;
- open a full listing page with photos, prices, rooms or ticket types, amenities, rules and ratings;
- save listings for later and share them.

Booking happens on bookhushly.com in the in-app browser until 4c.

**Done when:**

- On the Moto G06 against production, each category lists, searches, filters and opens correctly.
- Saved survives an app restart and works on a second device for the same account.
- No phone numbers or other private vendor fields are ever shown.
- `tsc`, lint and jest pass, and the Phase 4b phone checklist passes.

## 5. Error handling

- **Lists:** transient errors read "Couldn't load — try again" (neutral). A 429 keeps the shown items and retries after `retry_after`. A 400 or 500 shows a neutral error with retry.
- **Detail:** null → "This listing isn't available right now" (could be a suspended vendor, R2). Transient → `ErrorState`.
- **Saved writes:** optimistic with rollback. Stale session → neutral banner with **Sign in again**.

## 6. Accessibility and motion

- Targets ≥ 44 pt.
- The heart is a toggle with `accessibilityState.selected` and the label "Save <title>" or "Remove <title> from saved".
- Gallery images are labelled "Photo 2 of 12, <title>".
- Prices are read as words ("45,000 naira per night").
- Rails are scrollable lists with headings.
- Reduce Motion: no gallery parallax, and the heart doesn't bounce.

## 7. Testing

- **TDD for `discover.ts`:**
  - sanitising (every stripped character, the length cap);
  - params per category and filter (and that hotel bed/floor/date params are never produced);
  - price labels (hotel 0, early bird active/expired, free event, apartment rules);
  - sold out / low stock / ended / not on sale;
  - place map;
  - web URLs.
- **API tests:**
  - the listings call carries no `Authorization`;
  - detail column constants never include the forbidden fields (D4);
  - saved upsert/delete shapes and the zero-row guard.
- **RNTL:**
  - Explore (rails, Today banner, state chip);
  - Search (recent/popular, submit only);
  - Results (pagination, filter count, empty, 429);
  - Listing per kind (sold out, hidden listing, footer link);
  - Saved (toggle, undo, empty).
- No production calls from tests.
